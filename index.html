Platform agreement

Review the rules before joining.

Community conduct. Harassment, threats, sexual misconduct, stalking, discrimination, fraud, and repeated unwanted contact are prohibited. Confirmed violations will result in account removal. Credible threats or suspected criminal conduct will be referred to law enforcement where appropriate and permitted by law.

Lessons and meetings. Users are responsible for accurate listings and respectful communication. For in-person lessons, choose a public location and use your own judgment. Users are responsible for deciding whether a lesson or meeting is suitable.

Teacher responsibilities. Teachers set their own prices and are responsible for their own income reporting, taxes, permits, and other obligations under applicable local law. Learners and teachers agree lesson details directly.

Platform role. My Sign provides a place to discover tutors and exchange requests. To the extent permitted by law, My Sign is not responsible for independent user conduct or arrangements. Nothing in this agreement removes rights or liability that cannot legally be excluded.

Age. Members must be 18 or older.

Your country. Members choose their country when joining and can only see and contact tutors and learners in that same country.

Payments. Learners pay teachers directly (for example by Telebirr, CBE Birr, or bank transfer) after a teacher accepts a request. My Sign does not handle or hold this money. Teachers confirm payment on the site to unlock paid lesson videos.

Reports and suspension. Members can report other members. My Sign administrators review reports and may suspend or remove accounts that break these rules.

Prototype. Accounts, lessons, and requests are stored online. Booking notifications are not connected yet.

Privacy policy

Last updated: October 15, 2026

What we collect. When you join: your name, email address, password (stored scrambled, never readable), country, and role. If you add them: a profile picture, city or area, topics, a short bio, lessons and videos you publish, lesson requests and messages, and (for teachers) the payment details you choose to share. We also keep reports members send us.

Why we use it. To create your account, show you tutors and learners in your country, let you request, accept, and hold lessons, send account emails (like confirming your email or resetting your password), and keep the community safe.

Who can see what. Members in your country can see your name, profile picture, role, city, topics, and bio. Your email address is shared only with the other person in a lesson request (a teacher sees the learner's email; a learner sees the teacher's email after the teacher accepts). Teacher payment details are shown only to learners whose request the teacher accepted. Members in other countries cannot see you. Reports are seen only by My Sign administrators.

Services we use. Supabase stores accounts, lessons, and videos. Resend sends our emails. Video lessons happen on Jitsi Meet (meet.jit.si), which has its own privacy policy. The website is hosted on GitHub Pages and loads fonts from Google Fonts. These providers may store or process data outside Ethiopia, including in Europe and the United States.

Payments. My Sign does not collect or store card or bank logins. Learners pay teachers directly using the details the teacher provides.

How long we keep it. We keep your information while your account is open. When you delete your account, we delete your profile, lessons, videos, and requests, except where we must keep something to handle a safety report or meet a legal obligation.

Your choices and rights. You can see and edit your profile at any time in your dashboard, and delete your account yourself under My profile → Delete account. You can also ask us for a copy of your information, to correct it, or to delete your account by emailing support@accessedsolution.com.

Age. You must be 18 or older to join My Sign. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If we learn that a member is under 18, we will close the account.

Changes. If we change this policy, we will update the date above and tell members about important changes.

Contact. Questions or requests: support@accessedsolution.com.
